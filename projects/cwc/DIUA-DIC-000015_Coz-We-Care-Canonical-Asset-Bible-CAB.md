## Asset 005 — PrEP Bottle Illustration Specification

**Status:** CANONICAL / ACTIVE — Owner-approved on 2026-09-13 after controlled visual validation.

### Purpose

AS-005 defines the reusable CWC visual representation of a PrEP medicine bottle for educational content. It is a governed illustration asset, not a depiction of a specific commercial manufacturer or brand.

### Rendering Language

* Rendering direction must inherit the visual language of AS-002 CWC Official Brand Presenter: **CWC Stylized 3D Character Rendering**.
* AS-005 must not use an independent photorealistic product-rendering system if that creates visible style mismatch with the presenter.
* The bottle may retain realistic material cues, dimensionality, studio-like shading, and physically plausible form, but those cues must be simplified/stylized enough to remain visually coherent with the presenter.

### Core Visual Specification

* white medicine bottle body;
* white ribbed screw cap;
* teal/turquoise primary label field;
* clean, neutral or white presentation background for the master reference;
* front-facing or slight three-quarter product orientation unless content composition requires another permitted view;

### Contextual Presenter Styling Rule — OWNER-DIRECTED

The CWC Official Brand Presenter is a **generative brand character**, not a static sticker or fixed cut-out that must be copied unchanged from a canonical reference image.

For each content item, production should interpret the presenter in a way that supports the content's topic, audience, tone, narrative role, and composition. When useful, the presenter may be regenerated or restyled with context-appropriate:

* pose, gesture, facial expression, gaze, stance, and body orientation;
* wardrobe form, layering, accessories, and outfit styling, subject to the canonical CWC wardrobe-color rule;
* props, held objects, tools, educational objects, or other content-relevant accessories;
* activity, action, interaction, and scene role;
* crop, framing, camera angle, and composition role;
* situational or thematic styling that makes the presenter meaningfully participate in the content rather than appear as an unrelated pasted asset.

**Default production principle:** use the canonical GENREF set as **identity and rendering guidance**, not as a mandatory pixel source. Exact/raw reuse of a GENREF image is permitted only when it genuinely fits the composition; production must not treat copy-paste reuse as the default or as a fidelity requirement for AS-002.

It must preserve the canonical identity anchors, `CWC Stylized 3D Character Rendering`, and the current GENREF source hierarchy. A content-specific presenter derivative remains a **production derivative**. It does not become a new CAR asset, CRS reference, or canonical presenter state unless separately validated and promoted through the governed asset lifecycle.
