## 2. Core Editorial Principle

Historical recovered rule:

**One carousel = one learning objective.**

Supporting locked/default editorial principles recovered from the legacy Production Bible:

* One Slide, One Message;
* Progressive Learning;
* Empathy First;
* No Diagnostic / Shame Language;
* accessibility and readability;
* psychological explanation must support understanding rather than label or diagnose the reader.

The narrative objective is to help readers understand psychosocial experiences without stigmatizing or pathologizing them.

### 2.1 Named Psychology Standards — v1.0

The following named standards are adopted as Psychology Series production rules:

 1. **One Carousel, One Learning Objective Standard v1.0** — each carousel must serve one clearly defined learning objective.
 2. **Psychological Safety & Empathy Standard v1.0** — language must be non-judgmental, non-shaming, non-diagnostic, stigma-reducing, and grounded in realistic hope.
 3. **ODHIV-Centered Communication Standard v1.0** — Psychology Series framing remains centered on ODHIV lived context and peer-support relevance within the authority boundary defined in this Bible.
 4. **Evidence-Based Psychology Standard v1.0** — psychological explanation must be evidence-grounded and routed through the KDS Research Source List before being treated as verified.
 5. **Psychology Caption Framework v1.0** — caption structure: Hook → Insight → Empathy → Engagement CTA → Follow CTA.
 6. **Psychology Hashtag Standard v1.0** — exactly five hashtags in this order: #KDSSatuHatiSulawesiUtara; episode-topic hashtag; #Psikologi; #HIV; #ODHIV.
 7. **Psychology Episode Naming Standard v1.0** — sequential naming uses "Seri Psikologi N", optionally followed by the topic title.
 8. **Psychology Topic Reference Standard v1.0** — when a named psychological theory or model is materially used, the relevant theory/model and originating or primary scholarly attribution must be identified where editorially appropriate.
 9. **Psychology Visual Consistency Standard v1.0** — Psychology Series visual execution must remain consistent with current KDS visual authorities, this Bible, the Psychology Series Layout Bible, and registered assets.
10. **Psychology Reader Accessibility Standard v1.0** — copy must use clear language, explain necessary psychological terminology, and remain readable on mobile.

These names are labels for rules owned by this Production Bible; they do **not** create separate governance documents or competing authorities.
